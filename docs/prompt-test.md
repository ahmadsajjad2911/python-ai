# Prompt Test

**Prompt:** Suggest 3 AI solutions for a bakery that struggles with late delivery orders. Budget: 20000 PKR per month.

**Model:** gemma3:1b

**Answer:**
Here are three AI-based approaches that fit within 20,000 PKR/month (roughly $70 USD). I’ve kept them practical for a small bakery in Pakistan.

1. Delivery route optimization (about 3,000-6,000 PKR/month)
Late deliveries are often caused by riders taking inefficient routes or getting orders in a bad sequence. Route-planning tools such as Google Maps’ multi-stop planning, or affordable apps like Circuit or Routific, can sequence stops by distance and traffic. For a bakery with a few riders, this alone can cut delivery times noticeably. You can also batch nearby orders into one trip during busy periods like Eid, weddings, and evenings.

2. Order-volume forecasting for production planning (about 0-5,000 PKR/month)
Many late orders actually start in the kitchen: orders pile up, baking falls behind, and riders wait. Feed your past sales into a free or low-cost tool (Google Sheets with AI add-ons, or ChatGPT/Claude analysing your monthly sales export) to predict busy days and times. Then you can schedule bakers, pre-bake popular items, and set cutoff times for same-day orders. Accurate promised delivery times are also a fix in themselves, since customers feel “late” mostly when reality misses the promise.

3. AI chatbot for WhatsApp orders and customer updates (about 5,000-10,000 PKR/month)
Most Pakistani bakeries take orders over WhatsApp or Instagram, where mistakes, missed messages, and slow confirmations cause delays. A WhatsApp Business chatbot (via tools like Wati, Respond.io, or a simple Zapier/Make automation connected to an AI model) can take structured orders, confirm addresses, flag unavailable slots, and send automatic “order is out for delivery” updates. This reduces errors and cuts the back-and-forth that eats staff time, while keeping customers informed so complaints drop.

Suggested budget split

Route optimization: ~6,000 PKR
Forecasting/analysis: ~4,000 PKR
WhatsApp automation: ~10,000 PKR
Buffer: ~0-2,000 PKR

If you can only start with one, begin with route optimization if the delay happens after the food leaves the kitchen, or forecasting if orders are already late before dispatch. Tracking a simple log of where each late order lost time (kitchen, dispatch, or road) for two weeks will tell you which one to prioritize.

Prices for these tools change and are usually in USD, so check current plans before committing. If you tell me roughly how many orders you handle per day and how you deliver (own riders or third-party), I can tailor this further.
